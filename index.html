01 About

Business questions first, tools second.

I'm a Business Information Systems student at Benha University, building practical data analysis skills through hands-on projects. My process is the same each time: understand the question, check that the data can be trusted, then model it and design a dashboard that someone can act on.

I also try to be honest about what the data can't say. Some of my projects use training datasets with unrealistic patterns, and I flagged that in the write-ups instead of presenting the results as real findings.

I'm looking for a junior data analyst role or small freelance projects.

  1. STEP 01

    Frame the question

    Pin down the decision the analysis has to support before touching the data.

  2. STEP 02

    Validate the data

    Clean, de-duplicate and reconcile totals across tools so the numbers can be trusted.

  3. STEP 03

    Model it

    Star schemas, clear relationships and DAX measures with one agreed definition each.

  4. STEP 04

    Communicate

    Dashboards and write-ups that lead with the finding, the action, and the limits.
